DTG Atelier · Art-Direction Board

Pelican Parish

Designed before minted. This board is the drop's visual contract: the locked palette, the trait layer stack, and the exact metadata the chain will serve — rendered live from the same manifest the pipeline consumes.

01 · Palette

The Bayou Standard

Every hex the artwork may use, by role. Contrast ratios are computed live from these hexes against Bayou Ink and Heron White — the board and palette.md cannot drift apart.

Closed set. A hex not on this board is not in the drop. Tints and shades derived in the renderer are allowed; new base hexes are not.

02 · Trait Layers

The composition stack

Layers composite bottom-up by zIndex. Weights are positive integers summing to exactly 100 per layer. Dashed plate = optional layer — absence is an explicit None option pointing at a transparent PNG.

03 · Sample Metadata

What the chain will serve

A live draw from the manifest using the pipeline's rules: one seeded PRNG, serial weighted selection in zIndex order, uniqueness by combo hash. Same seed, same token — every time.
